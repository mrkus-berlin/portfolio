BiteMe.

brand builder for creators

Story

BiteMe. lets creators and influencers launch their own food and beverage products — without needing to find manufacturers, negotiate production, or manage logistics themselves. I co-founded the platform and led design across strategy, UX, interface, and brand direction.

Challenge

How might we let any creator launch their own branded food or beverage product — without the manufacturing, sourcing, or logistics expertise that usually stands in the way?

Outcome

A platform concept that connected creators directly with vetted producers, guiding them from product idea to a launch-ready brand in a handful of steps.

Team

1 Venture Developer
1 Online Marketer
1 Designer

My Stake

Product

You bring the idea. We bring the producers.

Creators had the audience and the idea, but launching a physical product meant navigating manufacturers, minimum order quantities, and e-commerce logistics alone — a barrier that stopped most before they started. BiteMe turns that into five guided steps, connecting creators directly with vetted producers and handling everything from customization to fulfillment, so the only thing they need to bring is the idea.

Learnings

Building BiteMe. surfaced just how much operational complexity sits behind a simple creator-facing idea — from vetting producers to managing production timelines. It was a valuable exercise in venture building end-to-end, from concept and brand to platform design, and shaped how I approach de-risking early-stage product bets since.
